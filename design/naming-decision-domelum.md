# Naming decision — **Domelum**

**Working lock:** Domelum  
**Descriptor:** Domelum — the household guide  
**Tagline (unchanged):** Everything they need while you are not there.

Screened as brand naming consultant after Notula FAIL. Full web + App Store (US/FR/GB) + primary domains — not App Store alone. Not a solicitor trademark opinion; buy domains and file UK/EU 9+42 this week.

---

## Why this one

The product is the house, held for the person who is not the owner. Latin *domus* = house. **Domelum** reads as a diminutive of that root: the little thing that stands for the house.

| Test | Result |
|---|---|
| "You should try Domelum." | Stands as a brand. No "the ___ of" |
| Spell from hearing | Yes — DOM-eh-lum / do-MEY-lum. No silent letters |
| EN / FR mouth | Same shape |
| Meaning | House — exact category, not a metaphor to explain |
| Dignity | Names the household, not the helper as servant |
| vs Notula | Same Latinate shelf; not already owned by AI notes |

**Story (memorability):** Domelum is from Latin *domus* — the house. You write the house once, with photos of the exact thing, and they ask it while you are not there.

**Mark:** keep the punctum (putty / petrol). It still reads as the smallest mark that holds the place.
