# Notula — data position (one page)

Plain English. For parents, agencies, and anyone who asks before they trust the product.
This is a product position, not legal advice — take proper advice before publishing.

---

## What we store

- The guide you write: notes, routines, contacts you choose to include
- Photos and short videos you attach (interiors, labelled objects, how-tos)
- Names and details of children, pets, and places **only as you write them**
- Who you have shared a guide with

We do not need your contacts list, microphone, or location to work.

## Where it lives

- **Today (v1):** guides live on the device that wrote them (local-first). Nothing is uploaded until a sync or share path exists and you choose it.
- **When sync/share ships:** data will be stored in [jurisdiction / provider — fill before launch], under a contract that keeps it in the UK/EEA or with an adequate transfer mechanism.

Update this paragraph the day a server holds a guide. Do not leave “local-first” as the public claim if that is no longer true.

## Who can see a guide

- Only people you give access to (link, invite, or agency placement you approve)
- When someone leaves (cleaner finishes, nanny moves on), you revoke access; their copy should stop working for new opens
- We do not sell guide content. We do not show it to other households

## Children

Guides often include children’s names, routines, and photos of their spaces. Under UK GDPR and the ICO Children’s Code, that data needs extra care. We will:

- Treat children’s data as sensitive by default
- Avoid profiling children for ads or engagement
- Make deletion of a child subject (and their media) a first-class action

## Photos of the home

Photos can reveal keys, alarm panels, floor plans, and valuables. The product will:

- Let you delete any photo and have it removed from storage
- Not use guide photos in marketing without separate, explicit consent
- Warn before sharing that the recipient will see those images

## AI / “Ask”

- Answers are grounded in **what you wrote in that guide**
- **Guide content is not used to train models.** Say this and mean it in the vendor contract
- Safety-critical facts (allergies, medication, emergency) are shown in your words, not rewritten by a model

## Retention and deletion

- You can delete a guide, a household, or the whole account
- Deletion removes associated photos from our storage within a defined window (state the number of days when sync exists)
- Backups expire on a published schedule
