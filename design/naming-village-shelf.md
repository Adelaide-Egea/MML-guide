# Naming — village / household / help shelf

Exploration after Notula FAIL. Theme brief: house, delegation, family, village, help, support, solidarity — Latin, Greek, and distillations of “it takes a village.”

**Method:** meaning first, then full clearance (web + App Store US/FR/GB + primary domains). App Store alone is not enough.

---

## 1. What the phrase gives you

“It takes a village to raise a child” is not a single ancient proverb with a clean source word. It is a *picture*: the child is held by more than one household.

| English bit | Useful Latin / Greek | Why it fits this product | Trap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Village | *vicus*, *vicinia* (the neighbors as a body), *κώμη* (*kōmē*) | The people who hold the house when you are not there | *Village* / Clinton book; **Vicinia** = live digital-twin software (`vicinia.io`) |
| House / household | *domus*, *familia*, *οἶκος* (*oikos*), *συνοικία* (*synoikia*) | The unit the guide is about | *Oikos* / *familia* crowded; *oiketēs* = household slave → dignity fail |
| Stand in for | *vicarius*, *vices*, *ἐπιτροπή* (*epitropē* = commission / guardianship) | Nanny / grandparent / cleaner as deputy | *Vicaria* soft; rhetoric sense of *epitrope* = conceding a point |
| Help / succour | *ops*, *opitulus* / *opitulator* (“bringer of help”), *auxilium*, *βοήθεια* | What the guide does for the second reader | *Auxilia* taken; **Boethia** ≈ Elder Scrolls *Boethiah* — fail |
| Of one another | *ἀλλήλων* (*allēlōn*), French *entraide* | Solidarity without hierarchy | *Allelon* = game + app; *Allelia* / *Allelica* = genomics |
| Steward of the house | *villicus* / *villica* (estate steward / housekeeper) | Exact role metaphor | Hierarchical; *villicus.com* old; dignity risk if it names the helper as servant |
| Household gods / hearth | *lares*, *penates*, *focus* / *foculus* (little hearth) | “What the house knows” | Religious / security apps on *Lares*; *foculus.com* old |
| Thing entrusted | *depositum*, *commissum*, *mandatum*, *traditum* | The guide itself | *Deposita.ai*, *Mandatum* banking, *Tutela* baby apps |

**Do not brand as “Village.”** The English word is a cliché shelf (Clinton, parenting blogs, charity). Take the *idea*, not the word.

## 3. Shortlist worth arguing (screened)

Clearance = App Store US/FR/GB quiet + no live same-spelling software found + domain note. **Not** a solicitor TM opinion.

| Candidate | Meaning | Brand test | Domains (RDAP) | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Opitulus** | Latin: helper / bringer of aid (also epithet of Jupiter) | “You should try Opitulus.” Soft but stands. Mouth: oh-PIT-yoo-lus (4 syllables — heavy) | `.com` `.app` `.io` `.co` look **free** | **Top of shelf** — cleanest clearance; meaning = help |
| **Epitropy** | From Greek *epitropē*: turning over / commission / guardianship | Exact product act: you hand the charge over | `.com` `.app` `.io` `.co` look **free** | **Strong** — delegation shelf; watch rhetoric sense of *epitrope* |
| **Alleleia** | Soft coinage from *allēlōn* (“of one another”) | Solidarity / village without saying village | all four look **free** | **Strong meaning**; soft risk near *allele* / Allelica |
| **Allelum** | Same root, shorter coinage | Same | all four look **free** | Lighter sister of Alleleia |
| **Vicula** | Latinate “little village / little street-quarter” | Closest literal village diminutive | `.com` taken (2024); `.app` free | Good meaning; buy/compound domain |
| **Villica** | Latin female estate / household steward | Role metaphor for the second reader | `.com` taken (2026); `.app` free | Meaning sharp; gendered + hierarchy risk |
| **Epitrope** | Classical form of the commission word | Clearer Greek than Epitropy | `.com` taken since 2005 | Prefer **Epitropy** unless `.com` acquired |
| **Foculus** | Little hearth | Atmosphere / house centre | `.com` 1998 + `.app` taken | Atmosphere only; skip |
| **Commissum** / **Traditum** | Thing entrusted / handed down | The artifact (like Notula was) | `.com` taken | Artifact shelf; weaker availability |

---

## 4. Recommendation

If the brief is **village / solidarity / help**, the cleanest locks to debate next are:

1. **Opitulus** — help (clearest web/store/domain screen today)  
2. **Epitropy** — the handover / charge turned over  
3. **Alleleia** or **Allelum** — “of one another” (village without *village*)

Pick one for solicitor classes 9/42 + handle buy before any product rename. Do not lock from this doc alone.

Visual system (putty / petrol / punctum) stays name-agnostic.
