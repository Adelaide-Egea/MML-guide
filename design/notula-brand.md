# Notula — brand system and launch plan

Decision document. Locked as the working brand direction pending Week 0 clearance (§8).

---

## 1. The name

**Notula** — Latin, the diminutive of *nota*. The little note.

| Test | Result |
|---|---|
| "You should try Notula." | Reads as a brand. Stands alone, no "the ___ of" |
| Spell from hearing | Yes. No silent letters, no Z/X ambiguity |
| Second reader | Transparent to Portuguese, Spanish, Italian, Polish speakers via *nota* |
| Metaphor to explain | None. It is the artifact, not a picture of it |
| French mouth | No friction. no-TU-la |
| Namespace | App Store exact-match clear (noise 1: near-hit `Notu.la`). A desktop Markdown tool also uses the word — **clearance still required — see §8** |

**Why it beat the others:** every Latin word with a pretty image attached (Tessera, Vesta, Lumen, Nexus, Epitome-as-aspiration) is crowded or elevated. The uncrowded shelf is Latin words whose meaning is administratively dull. That shelf is also the correct one — this product *is* stationery.

**Rejected / superseded for this decision:** Epitome (elevated), Tessera (crowded), Tabella (taken — see §8), Understudy, Consigne, Holdall, Bento, Household, coined soft names, metaphor-first round five.

---

## 2. The brand idea

**One line:** Everything they need while you are not there.

**The recommendation sentence:** "You should try Notula. You write it once, with photos, and they can just ask it."

**The enemy:** the long text message. Sent at 8:40am, half-read, wrong in three places, resented by both sides.

**Category position:** not a chore app, not a family organiser, not a marketplace. Notula is **the handover.** One household, one guide, handed to whoever is holding it today.

**What Notula is not:**
- Not surveillance. No tracking, no completion policing, no reporting back to you.
- Not "mum life". No pastel, no cursive, no exclamation marks.
- Not a task list you assign. It is a reference they consult.

**The dignity rule.** The second reader is a competent adult doing a job. Every word in the product treats them that way. This is the single thing that will differentiate Notula from every competitor, and it is a brand decision, not a copy decision.

## 3. Voice

| Rule | Example |
|---|---|
| Short declaratives | "The bins go out Tuesday night." |
| You address the writer. The guide addresses the reader. | App to you: "Add a photo." Guide to her: "The spare key is here." |
| Photos replace adjectives | Never "the good blue cloth". Show it. |
| No apology, no hedging | Not "if you could just...". "Tuesday night." |
| Never cute | No emoji in product chrome. Emoji in her content is her choice. |

## 4. Identity

**The mark: the punctum.** A small square sitting on a rule. It is the smallest mark you can make, which is what the name means. It is not a checkbox, not a mosaic, not a picture-in-picture icon, and it does not read as any other app category.

| Asset | Spec |
|---|---|
| App icon | Putty tile `#e0d2bc`, radius 22% of tile, punctum + rule in ink `#2c2721`, optically centred (~4% above true centre) |
| Two-tone | Rule in petrol `#306369`, square in ink. Marketing and web only |
| Reversed | Ink tile, mark in putty. Dark mode and favicon |
| Wordmark | Newsreader Regular, "Notula" + punctum as terminal square |
| Minimum size | Mark 16px. Below that, punctum only, no rule |
| Clear space | One punctum-width on all sides |

**Do not:** outline the mark, add a gradient, rotate the punctum, put the wordmark inside the tile, or place the mark on raspberry.

### Colour

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Putty | `#e0d2bc` | Icon, marketing surfaces, dividers, edges |
| Card / in-app ground | `#efe7da` | **In-app ground.** Full-bleed putty reads heavy and dated at scale |
| Elevated surface | `#faf6f0` | Cards sitting on the in-app ground |
| Ink | `#2c2721` | All primary text and the mark |
| Muted | `#4f4a42` | Secondary text. Darkened so 13px holds in a dim utility room |
| Petrol | `#306369` | The single interactive accent (`--brand`) |
| Raspberry | `#d9577e` | Done state only. Dots, ticks, rules. Never a fill or background |
| Critical | `#a92e2c` | Safety only, and never colour alone — always paired with an icon or word |

Lisette blue `#445f72` is **retired** as an interactive accent. It sat too close to petrol and diluted both.

### Type

|---|---|---|

| UI + body | Figtree | Body **17px** |
| Timeline times | Figtree | Tabular numerals on |

## 5. The pitch (say this out loud)

> Every household runs on knowledge that lives in one person's head. Where the spare key is. Which cloth is for the pan. That the little one will say she's allowed screens after dinner and she isn't.
>
> When you leave, you try to transfer all of it in a text message on the way out of the door. It doesn't work. She forgets, or she guesses, or she calls you in a meeting. So you either micromanage or you come home to a routine you don't recognise.
>
> Notula is the guide you write once. Photos of the exact thing, so there's no interpreting. A timeline, so she knows what matters when. And she can ask it questions and get the answer back in her own language, without asking you.
>
> You stop repeating yourself. She stops guessing. Nobody has to be managed.

## 6. Go to market — six weeks, gated

Each week has one gate. **Do not proceed past a failed gate — change the product instead.**

### Week 0 — Clear the name
Nothing else gets designed until this is done. Details in §8.
**Gate:** Notula is clear in UK/EU class 9 and 42, and a domain is bought.

### Week 1 — The handover test
Recruit **8 households** who have a real absence in the next 14 days.

| Metric | Pass |
|---|---|
| Guide completed and actually handed over | 6 of 8 |
| **Second reader opened it unprompted, more than once** | 5 of 8 |
| Time to write the first guide | Under 25 minutes |

### Week 2 — Fix the cold start
Starter templates, sample household, photo-first entry.
**Gate:** first-guide time under 15 minutes with a new cohort of 5.

### Week 3 — Test the second reader's language
Real non-English speakers (PT/BR, PL, ES, RO, Tagalog in London).
**Gate:** 4 of 5 second readers prefer it to texting you.

### Week 4 — Charge
Free: one guide, one person. Paid: unlimited. Anchor annual to one hour of a cleaner's time per year.
**Gate:** 15% of week 1–3 testers pay.

### Week 5–6 — Referral, then launch
**Gate:** 3 unprompted referrals from 20 testers before paid ads.

## 7. Channels, ranked

1. Agencies and placers (nanny, cleaning, dog boarders, holiday-let managers)
2. Holiday lets and pet sitting — fastest revenue wedge
3. Where the buyer already talks (local WhatsApp, NCT, Mumsnet local)
4. School and nursery offices

Deprioritise Product Hunt and tech Twitter.
